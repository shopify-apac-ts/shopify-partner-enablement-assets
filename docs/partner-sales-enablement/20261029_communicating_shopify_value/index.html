Partner sales webinar

Communicating Shopify value in the first sales meeting

29 October 2026 · Online

This first Partner Sales Enablement session helps partner sales teams move beyond a list of Shopify features and explain why Shopify matters to a specific customer. It covers common positioning pitfalls, how to connect capabilities to customer needs, how to tailor the first conversation and how to organize a clear “Why Shopify” for an active opportunity.

Audience
Sales representatives at Shopify partner companies who lead discovery, first meetings and proposals.
