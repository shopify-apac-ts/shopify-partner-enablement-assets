Shopify migration essentials

17 September 2026 · 15:00–17:00 JST · Online

Practical guidance for partners supporting a migration to Shopify. The session covers how to clarify business challenges and existing requirements, explain the value of Shopify Plus, conduct discovery, and identify the key considerations for migration. Sales and technical teams explore an assessment approach that connects proposals, discovery and delivery. Use the slides and assessment worksheet to organize a target project's needs, prepare follow-up discussions and develop your own migration support services.

Audience
Sales and presales engineers / solution architects, ideally attending as a sales and technical pair. Also recommended for commerce consultants and delivery / project leads.
Presenters
